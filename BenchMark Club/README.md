# The Flax Benchmark Club.
Want to join the club?  It's simple.  Download the **speedtest.eflax** file and run it in Flax on your computer, Linux or macOS.  Once complete, send us a screenshot of your terminal, a nickname for yourself and your machine spec and OS then we'll include your score here ranked along with others.  **[Download the Speed Tester](blob:https://github.com/38926446-43b4-4cc5-92b8-0cbbba14177e?download=1)**

Hopefully we can get a real competition started.  Email your results to **[The Flax Project](mailto:theflaxproject@icloud.com)**
