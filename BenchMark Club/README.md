# The Flax Benchmark Club.
Want to join the club?  It's simple.  Download the **Speed Tester** and run the **eflax** file in Flax on your computer, Linux or macOS.  Once complete, send us a screenshot of your terminal, a nickname for yourself and your machine spec and OS then we'll include your score here ranked along with others.  **[Download the Speed Tester.](https://github.com/ryncarmichael/Flax/raw/main/BenchMark%20Club/speedtest.zip?download=1)**

Hopefully we can get a real competition started.  Email your results to **[The Flax Project.](mailto:theflaxproject@icloud.com)**
